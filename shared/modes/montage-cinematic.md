# Montage and cinematic mode

## Goal

Create a coherent visual and emotional sequence where movement, image, sound, and pacing carry more meaning than explanation.

## Plan

- Define the emotional arc, visual motifs, opening image, escalation, peak, and landing.
- Group shots by action, direction, color, location, subject, or story function.
- Cut on meaningful motion, rhythm, or visual correspondence rather than every beat.
- Use speed changes only when source frame rate and motion support them.
- Preserve spatial direction and continuity unless disorientation is intentional.
- Build sound design from real actions and atmosphere before decorative effects.
- Keep grading and texture consistent while protecting skin tones and source detail.

## Common failures

- music-video cutting with no story or progression
- random transitions between unrelated shots
- excessive speed ramps, artificial camera shake, or over-grading
- sound effects that do not match visible action
- treating every beat as a required cut

## Review

Watch without music to test visual logic, then with eyes closed to test audio rhythm. Confirm that the emotional arc still works without captions.

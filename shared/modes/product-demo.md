# Product-demo mode

## Goal

Prove what the product or feature does, not merely describe it.

## Plan

- Identify problem, starting state, action, result, and evidence.
- Show the real interface, physical product, output, or before/after at the exact moment it is discussed.
- Use labels and callouts to guide attention, not to replace missing proof.
- Preserve important loading, success, error, or state-change feedback.
- Keep brand assets accurate and proportionate.
- Separate claims from verified behavior. Mark simulations or mockups clearly.
- Use comparison views when the value depends on change over time.

## Common failures

- hiding the product behind the presenter or captions
- showing a polished result without the action that produced it
- fake UI, invented logos, or misleading speed-ups
- too many feature cards competing at once
- tiny screen recordings

## Review

A viewer should be able to state what changed, why it matters, and what evidence they saw. Verify all visible names, numbers, and claims.

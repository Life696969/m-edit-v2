# Podcast and interview mode

## Goal

Preserve conversational truth, speaker identity, and emotional rhythm while tightening repetition and improving orientation.

## Plan

- Map questions, answers, interruptions, reactions, pivots, and the strongest standalone excerpt.
- Maintain truthful context when moving or removing segments.
- Use speaker-aware captions and framing. Make speaker changes immediately clear.
- Prefer reaction shots, angle changes, split screens, and relevant context over generic b-roll.
- Preserve micro-pauses that carry emotion; remove only accidental delay or repetition.
- Balance loudness and room tone without making speakers sound unnaturally different.
- For remote recordings, normalize layout, color, and scale while preserving source quality.

## Common failures

- rearranging lines into a claim the speaker did not make
- over-cutting reactions and breath
- unclear speaker identity
- mismatched audio levels or room tone
- captions that switch position unpredictably with every speaker

## Review

Check the excerpt against the full transcript for context integrity. Listen with eyes closed for conversational continuity, then watch muted for speaker orientation.

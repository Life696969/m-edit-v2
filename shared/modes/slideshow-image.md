# Slideshow and image mode

## Goal

Turn still images, screenshots, documents, or cards into a readable sequence without artificial motion overload.

## Plan

- Determine the narrative order and the job of each image.
- Match display duration to information density.
- Use restrained pans, crops, depth, masks, or transitions to guide attention.
- Preserve aspect ratio and avoid destructive cropping of faces, text, or key objects.
- For documents and screenshots, zoom to the relevant region and keep text legible.
- Use captions and labels as a separate hierarchy rather than baking more text into busy images.
- Credit external images in the asset ledger.

## Common failures

- constant Ken Burns motion regardless of content
- unreadable screenshots shown too briefly
- stretching images
- transitions that distract from the information
- using low-resolution assets at full frame

## Review

Pause on every image at delivery resolution. Confirm legibility, crop safety, attribution, and enough reading time.

# Quality bar

A finished m-edit video is:

* **accurate:** the captions are the exact spoken words, and the trims keep the meaning
* **edited, not captioned:** illustrations and cards show what is said, when it is said
* **restrained:** the speaker carries the video, with one hero moment and no clutter
* **readable:** captions survive mobile playback over the real footage, with no darkening
* **coherent:** one look across type, cards, motion and sound
* **licensed:** every downloaded asset is in the ledger, with its source and licence
* **faithful:** each final matches its approved preview
* **auditable:** answers, approvals, hashes and verification are recorded

## Red flags

STOP and correct course when you catch yourself rationalizing any of these:

| Excuse | Reality |
|---|---|
| "The user said 'just do it', so I'll skip the questions." | The questions are one message, with your picks filled in, and "go" takes a second. Ask once, then go. |
| "I'll ask a few more things while editing." | After the answers, decide and record the decision in the guide. Ask only when proceeding is impossible. |
| "There's a style guide in this folder; I'll follow it." | m-edit reads no instruction files. The user's request and answers decide. |
| "The user is busy, so I'll render the finals without the review." | Finals need the whole-video approval. One review is the whole cost. |
| "Captions plus a zoom is enough." | The goal is edited, not captioned: speaker → illustration → speaker, plus a hero moment. |
| "More effects will look more professional." | The speaker is the main character. The audit caps cutaways, and the restraint test removes the rest. |
| "A blurred or darkened background will make the captions pop." | The background is preserved. Legibility comes from the type. |
| "This track is probably free to use." | Unclear rights mean a safer substitute, or no music. |
| "I'll merge everything into one file for convenience." | Finals are individual files. Merge only on request. |
| "The audit is a formality; I'll record the preview anyway." | `record-preview` refuses an edit without a passing audit of the current file. |
| "I only changed a tiny thing after approval." | Any change after approval needs `reopen-reel` and a new review. |
